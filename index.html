About Formulators Hub

Formulators Hub Uganda is East Africa's premier cosmetic science and product development firm, dedicated to transforming raw ideas into extraordinary skincare, haircare, and fragrance products.

Founded by passionate cosmetic chemists, we blend rigorous science with deep knowledge of African botanical ingredients — creating formulations that are safe, effective, and culturally resonant.

Whether you are launching your first product line or scaling an established brand, we provide end-to-end solutions from lab bench to market shelf.

Mission

To democratise cosmetic science by making expert formulation accessible to every African entrepreneur, brand, and beauty professional.

Vision

To be the foremost centre of cosmetic innovation on the African continent, shaping global beauty standards by 2030.

Values

Scientific rigour · Ethical sourcing · Client-centred innovation · Pan-African pride · Transparency in every formulation.
